Snake Brawl Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026 (Wi-Fi play only; update check)

Snake Brawl ("the game") is made by RanEddie Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, where it is stored and who can see it. It applies to the Android app and to the browser version of the game.

1. Summary

  • We do not require an account, email address, phone number or real name.
  • We do not show ads and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • Your progress, nickname and age are stored only on your own device.
  • There is no internet multiplayer. When you play with friends on the same Wi-Fi, some game information is shared directly with their devices on your local network, as described below.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following data locally on your device (in the app's private storage on Android, or in your browser's local storage in the browser version):

  • Your age, entered when you first start the game. It is used only on your device, to turn on protections for younger players: players under 13 choose a generated nickname instead of typing one.
  • A list of completed purchase tokens from Google Play, so a purchase is never granted twice.
  • Your nickname. Players 13 and over may type one (we ask players not to use their real name); younger players pick from generated names such as "SlyRattler96".
  • Game progress: coins, trophies, brawlers, skins, upgrade levels, Snake Pass progress, club choice and statistics.
  • Maps you draw in the map maker, redeem codes you have used, a random player ID (used only to check codes made for you), and daily reward and quest progress.
  • Your settings (sound, vibration, graphics, controls).

This data is not sent to us or to any server. You can delete it at any time with "Reset progress" in the game settings, by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app, or by clearing your browser's site data.

3. Clubs

Clubs are local to your device. The other club members are computer-controlled players. Your club name and badge are stored only on your device.

4. Playing with friends

Wi-Fi play (Android app, all ages, up to 3 devices). Your device connects directly to your friend's device on the same local network. The game announces your room on the local network so nearby devices running Snake Brawl can find it. Your nickname, selected brawler, skin colors, power level, trophy count and in-game controls are sent to your friend's device. Nothing is sent over the internet.

The browser version has no multiplayer.

Update check (Android app). When the game starts it downloads a small public text file from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) that says which game version is the newest, so it can tell you when an update is available. No information about you or your game is sent; like any download, GitHub sees your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Sharing. If you tap "Invite friends" or "Share" on a map, the game opens your device's own share menu with a short text (an invite or a map code). You choose where it goes; the game does not send it anywhere itself.

5. Purchases

The Android app sells optional coin packs. Purchases are made and processed entirely by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policy. We never receive your name, email address or payment details: the game only gets a confirmation from Google Play that a purchase was completed, which it uses on your device to add the coins. All coins can also be earned by playing. Parents can require approval for purchases with Google Play's parental controls and Google Family Link. The browser version has no purchases.

6. Permissions (Android)

The Android app requests only these permissions, which do not show a prompt:

  • Network access: Android needs this permission for the Wi-Fi connection between phones in the same room, for Google Play purchases, and to check for updates (see section 4).
  • Wake lock: keeps Wi-Fi fast during a match with friends.
  • Wi-Fi state and multicast: to find and announce rooms on your local Wi-Fi network for Wi-Fi play.

The game does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

7. Children's privacy

The game is suitable for players of all ages and is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We do not collect personal information from children: their data stays on their device, they cannot type a free-text nickname, and there is no online play or chat with strangers. In-app purchases go through Google Play, where parents can require approval for every purchase. Parents can delete all game data at any time as described in section 2. If you believe a child's information has been shared, contact us and we will help.

8. Data security

Because your data stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security. Wi-Fi play only works between devices on the same local network.

9. Your rights

We do not hold any personal data about you on our side. You control all game data on your device and can delete it at any time as described in section 2.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The new version will be published here with a new effective date.

11. Contact
